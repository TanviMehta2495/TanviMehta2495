# Publish your GitHub profile story (5 minutes)

1. On GitHub, click **New repository** and name it exactly **TanviMehta2495**, the same as your username. Make it **Public** and tick "Add a README".
2. Upload everything in this folder (`README.md` and the whole `assets/` folder). The easiest way: **Add file → Upload files**, then drag the files in.
3. Commit. Your story now shows at the top of https://github.com/TanviMehta2495

## Add your photos
- Put your photos in `assets/photos/` (e.g. `roots.jpg`, `bangalore.jpg`, `dallas.jpg`, `today.jpg`). Landscape photos around 1200 px wide work best.
- In README.md, change `assets/photos/roots.svg` to `assets/photos/roots.jpg` (and the same for the others).
- Don't use screenshots of internal company systems or company logos.

## Personal touches (search for ✍️ in README.md)
There are two spots where one line in your own voice will make the story land: why you chose engineering, and how the move to Dallas felt.
